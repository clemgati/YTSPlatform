# Product Vision

Yellow Track Platform exists to remove friction from creative work.

Creative professionals spend too much time switching between disconnected
applications, searching for information, repeating manual tasks, and managing
their business instead of practicing their craft.

Yellow Track Platform brings those workflows together into one thoughtful,
cross-platform experience.

Our goal is not to become another photography application.

Our goal is to become the operating system that supports the entire creative
workflow.

---

# Mission

Remove friction from creative work so creators can spend more time creating.

---

# Vision

Build the operating system for modern photographers and creative professionals.

---

# Who We Build For

Yellow Track Platform is designed for creators who care deeply about their craft.

Our users value:

- Thoughtful workflows
- Beautiful tools
- Preparation
- Organization
- Continuous improvement
- Craftsmanship

Today our primary audience is photographers and videographers.

Tomorrow, the platform should be flexible enough to grow into adjacent creative
disciplines without losing its identity.

---

# Product Principles

## Creator First

Every feature must improve the creative workflow.

If it doesn't meaningfully help creators, it doesn't belong.

---

## Workflow Before Features

We don't build isolated features.

We build complete workflows.

Every feature should naturally connect to the rest of the platform.

---

## Calm Software

The application should reduce stress.

Interfaces should feel clean, intentional, and focused.

The software should never compete for the user's attention.

---

## Intelligence Without Intrusion

Automation should quietly reduce repetitive work.

Artificial intelligence should assist—not distract.

The platform should remember so creators don't have to.

---

## Build for the Long Term

Technology changes.

Workflows endure.

Every decision should support the platform we want to maintain years from now.

# What We Will Not Build

Yellow Track Platform is not trying to replace:

- Lightroom
- Photoshop
- Final Cut Pro
- DaVinci Resolve

These are exceptional creative tools.

Instead, Yellow Track Platform connects the workflows surrounding them.
